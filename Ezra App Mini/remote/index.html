App

Go away / come back just show or hide her overlay window (same as the tray's "Show Ezra" checkbox). Exit Ezra quits the whole app, including this Remote window.

Behaviors

Interrupts whatever she's doing and queues the clicked behavior next. "Wander off-screen" uses the same randomized away-duration as the real thing (see engine.js WANDER_AWAY_MS) -- use Skip to call her back early instead of waiting it out.

Experimental (not in V1)

Skips straight to the strike sequence (watch, then the swipe loop) wherever she is right now, for art/feel preview only. Not part of V1mini and not on the automatic idle loop -- the real cursor-triggered version (resting your cursor near her for 2+ seconds) is intentionally switched off for this build.

Pose preview

Freezes her on a single frame for art review, independent of the behavior loop.
