Ezra is drawn from a real cat — one who used to sit exactly like this, in exactly this kind of light, in a home that felt a little warmer for having her in it. She isn't with us anymore.

This app is a small, stubborn way of keeping a piece of her around: her colors, her collar, her particular way of watching the room like she was in charge of it. If she makes you smile for a second while she ignores you completely, that's the idea. That's who she was.

Made with love, for her — and now, for your desktop.

What She Actually Does

A full-time employee of doing whatever she wants

Ezra has a rich inner life that does not include you. Here's what that looks like from the outside.

Sits. Judges.

Her default state. She parks herself in your corner and radiates quiet, unearned authority over your entire workspace.

Glances up (sometimes at flies)

Every so often she snaps her attention to something above her. There is never anything there. She does not care that you know this.

Stretches, wanders, saunters back

Every now and then she gets up, has a full-body stretch and a yawn about it, wanders off-screen for a bit, and then reappears like nothing happened.

Walks with purpose (unknown to you)

She has places to be. You will never know where. It is almost certainly nowhere.

Naps, with slow breathing

She curls up and drifts off with a gentle rise-and-fall breathing animation. Statistically, this is what she'll be doing when you check on her.

Flicks her tail at you

A tiny, precise tail flick that means something. We don't know what. Neither will you. That's the deal with cats.

Grooms herself, mid-task

She will stop whatever she's doing — which is nothing — to groom herself, because personal maintenance takes priority over you, always.

Blinks, at her own pace

Slow, deliberate, occasionally aimed in your general direction. In cat terms this is basically a hug. Don't get used to it.

Exists. Beautifully. Nearby.

Lightweight, quiet, and always exactly as present as she feels like being. No feeding required. Yet.
