Ezra is a very small, very orange cat who has claimed a corner of your screen and has no intention of discussing it further. She sits, she wanders, she naps, she judges. Mostly you.

She's not around to see this page. The app doesn't seem to know that, and honestly, neither do we. She lives on here anyway — try to keep up.

What She Does (Loosely)

Mostly she sits and radiates quiet, unearned authority over your screen. Every so often she'll stretch through a full yawn, glance sharply at something that isn't there, wander off mid-thought, and saunter back like nothing happened — all without your input, consent, or applause.
